How to Use TallyDataX

Follow this simple guide to extract live vouchers from Tally Prime into pixel-perfect Excel accounting reports with party-wise totals, GSTIN, full address, and auto-calculated formulas.

Important: Date Range is Controlled Directly from Tally Prime

Key Rule

TallyDataX extracts data for the exact Period currently active/open in your Tally Prime. Before extracting, set your desired From Date and To Date in Tally Prime. Whatever date range is set in Tally will be extracted 100% accurately and at maximum speed.

Keyboard Shortcut in Tally: Alt + F2 → Enter "From Date" & "To Date" (e.g. 01-Apr-2025 to 31-Mar-2026)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Guide

Essential answers to common questions for seamless accounting & audit

8 Important FAQs
1 How to connect Tally Prime with TallyDataX?

Make sure Tally Prime is running on your computer with your company open. In Tally, navigate to F1: Help → Settings → Connectivity → Client/Server configuration and configure TallyPrime acts as: Both and Port: 9000. Save settings and restart Tally if prompted.

2 How to extract data for a specific date range or financial year?

You do not need to enter any dates inside the program! Simply press Alt + F2 inside Tally Prime to set your desired period (e.g. 01-04-2024 to 31-03-2026). TallyDataX automatically extracts all vouchers within that active period.

3 How to merge data across multiple financial years?

Go to Tab 3 "3. Excel File Merger". Drag and drop your exported Excel files from different financial years and click "Start Consolidation & Merge". The system merges all vouchers for each party chronologically (date-wise) into a unified master report.

4 Is my Tally data safe? Can this software alter or delete records?

100% Safe and Strictly Read-Only. TallyDataX only reads (extracts) data via Tally's official HTTP XML interface. It has zero capability to alter, modify, create, or delete any vouchers, ledgers, or settings in your company.

5 Will 50,000+ or 100,000+ vouchers slow down my computer?

Not at all. TallyDataX includes an Ultra-Fast 500-Record DOM Capping Engine to ensure your browser remains fast and lag-free, while the exported Excel file includes all 50,000+ complete records with active formulas and subtotals.

6 Does this program require an active Internet connection?

No. TallyDataX runs 100% offline on your local computer (127.0.0.1:8000). Your private financial data never leaves your computer and is never sent to any external server or cloud.

7 How do Freeze Panes and live Excel formulas work?

In both exported and merged Excel workbooks, Row 1 (Header row) is permanently frozen so headers remain visible when scrolling. Additionally, live formulas are included for Invoice Amount (=J+N), Party Subtotals (=SUM), and Net Balance (=O-P).

8 Where are Party GSTIN, Address, State, and PIN extracted from?

The extractor pulls GSTIN, street address, state, and pincode directly from ledger masters, voucher overrides, and mailing details. If state was left blank in Tally, it automatically resolves the standard state name using the 2-digit GSTIN prefix.
